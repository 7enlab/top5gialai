Top 5 Places to Stay in Pleiku by Budget for Festival Visitors

The Gong Festival - Wild Sunflower Week runs 30/10-8/11/2026, and visitor flow continues through later events into December, according to the niche profile of 24/10/2026. Book early, and read prices the following way: this article does not fix a single Vietnamese-dong price per hotel, because booking-platform prices change by date, room type and display currency. Each place below records exactly what the platform showed on 29/10/2026, in its original currency, so readers can re-check for their own nights. Festival-week prices may be higher than the off-peak nights quoted here, and rooms may sell out early.

How the list was chosen

A place made the list when it met all three conditions: a central or near-central Pleiku location convenient for festival events, a public booking-platform record with an address and a price retrievable on 29/10/2026, and a different budget tier from the others so readers can choose by wallet. The order below runs from budget to premium; it is not a quality ranking.

1. Pleiku Hotel by Gia Lai Tourist - budget

Address as listed by platforms: 03 Nguyen Tat Thanh, Pleiku. This 3-star hotel has the lowest price found in this round: Expedia lists GBP 17 per night for 18-19 Nov, taxes and fees included. Its advantage for festival visitors is a central location under the Gia Lai Tourist brand, a familiar local travel company. The caveat: that price is for one specific night in November, not a fixed price for festival week, so re-check for your exact dates.

2. Pleiku & Em Hotel by Gia Lai Tourist - budget, breakfast included

Address as listed by platforms: 86 Nguyen Van Troi, Pleiku, 0.51 km from the city centre according to Wotif. Expedia lists GBP 19 per night for 4-5 Nov, taxes and fees included, with reserve-now-pay-later and free cancellation. Wotif describes it as a guest house with free breakfast and free Wi-Fi. For festival mornings, breakfast included in the room is a real saving, since mornings heading to events leave little time to find a restaurant. As with place 1, the price applies to the night quoted, not to festival week as a whole.

3. Khanh Linh Hotel - lower mid-range

Address as listed by platforms: 232 Nguyen Tat Thanh, Pleiku. Hotels.com rates it 3 stars, 8.0/10 from 25 reviews, and lists a lowest price found in the past 24 hours of CNY 245 per night, CNY 278 total including taxes and fees for 11-12 Nov. The hotel has parking, a plus for visitors driving the Pleiku - Bien Ho - Chu Dang Ya route. The price is given in the platform's display currency and is not converted here, since exchange rates move daily.

4. Le Centre Pleiku Hotel - mid-range

Le Centre is a 3-star hotel in central Pleiku. Trip.com lists rates from USD 19 per night, check-in from 14:00 and check-out until 12:00, with Pleiku night market 0.24 km away. Agoda lists an average room rate of USD 41 per night, against a Pleiku-wide average of USD 27 per night. The two figures do not contradict each other: one is a starting rate, the other an average. For festival visitors, Le Centre sits in the middle of the Pleiku market - neither the cheapest nor the premium option. Its street number could not be cross-checked in the 29/10/2026 round, so this article records the central area rather than a specific address; confirm the street number on the booking platform before travelling.

5. Muong Thanh Grand Gia Lai - the premium pick in this list

Address as listed by platforms: 537 Hung Vuong, Pleiku, Phu Dong area. This 3.5-star hotel scores 8.3 from 96 reviews on Traveloka, with massage services, babysitting and car rental. Agoda lists room rates from AUD 54 per night; Traveloka lists around PHP 1,930 per night. The two figures are in different currencies for different check dates, so they are not merged into one number here. This is the pick for visitors wanting full facilities and multi-night stays, or families needing babysitting and on-site car rental.

Quick pick

Travelling solo or as a couple on a tight budget: start with Pleiku Hotel or Pleiku & Em, both Gia Lai Tourist properties in the centre. Driving yourself and needing parking: Khanh Linh. Wanting the centre near the night market: Le Centre. Travelling with family for several days and needing facilities: Muong Thanh Grand Gia Lai. Whichever you choose, book a free-cancellation rate during festival week if one is still available, and screenshot the price and cancellation terms on the day you book, since festival-week prices move faster than the off-peak rates quoted here.
